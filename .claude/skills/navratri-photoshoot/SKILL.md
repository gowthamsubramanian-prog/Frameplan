# Navratri 9-Day Photoshoot

Use this when the user wants Navratri / Garba / Durga Puja photoshoot image prompts. It supplies the themes; the prompt-writing rules come from the `ultra-realistic-portrait` skill (load it too if available). The essentials are repeated here so this skill works on its own.

## How to build each prompt

Every shot is a separate, standalone prompt, assembled in this order:

1. **Identity lock:** "Create an ultra-realistic photo using my uploaded reference photo as the SOLE identity reference. Preserve the exact same person: face, facial features, skin tone, body type and natural appearance. Do not beautify, slim, de-age or alter her identity."
2. **Look:** the day's outfit + jewelry & beauty (repeat in full every shot).
3. **Setting & light:** the day's setting and lighting.
4. **Pose:** the shot's pose.
5. **Camera:** close-up 85mm f/1.8, half-body 85mm f/2, three-quarter 50mm f/2.2, full-body 50mm f/2.8.
6. **Realism tail:** "Real DSLR photography, natural skin texture, true-to-life fabric, mirror-work and embroidery detail, accurate hands with five fingers, no plastic or airbrushed look, no text, no watermark, single image, not a collage."

Never add grid, collage or frame-position instructions.

## Workflow

1. Ask which day(s) the user wants (one day, several, or all 9) unless they said. Default: all 4 shots of each chosen day.
2. If they want to match this year's official Navratri colour calendar (it changes yearly), offer to swap the outfit colours while keeping the garment type and theme.
3. Output each shot as a heading (e.g. "Day 3 - Shot 2 - Aarti Thali") followed by the full prompt in its own code block.
4. End with: attach the same reference photo to every prompt and generate one image at a time.

## Day 1 - Shailaputri - Marigold Morning

- **Outfit:** Mustard-yellow and marigold-orange Gujarati bandhani lehenga with gota-patti border, red bandhani odhni with gold lace.
- **Jewelry & beauty:** Gold temple jhumkas, small maang tikka, gold nose pin, red and gold glass bangles, small red bindi. Fresh dewy makeup, soft kohl.
- **Setting & light:** Sunrise courtyard decorated with marigold torans and a brass kalash for Ghatasthapana with sprouting jawara in a clay bowl. Soft golden sunrise light.
- **Shot 1 - Close-up Kalash:** Holding a small brass kalash with mango leaves near her face, soft smile, direct eye contact.
- **Shot 2 - Half-body Toran:** Tying a marigold toran at a carved wooden doorway, looking back over her shoulder at camera.
- **Shot 3 - Full-body Twirl:** Mid-twirl with the lehenga flared in a full circle, odhni floating, joyful expression.
- **Shot 4 - Seated Ghatasthapana:** Seated cross-legged beside the kalash and jawara, both hands offering marigold petals, peaceful expression.
